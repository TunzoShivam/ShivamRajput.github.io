Research

My research is in intelligent reflecting surface (IRS/RIS) assisted wireless communication, carried out at IIT Mandi under Dr. Pavan Kumar Reddy Manne as part of my M.Tech (Research) + Ph.D. dual degree. Research interests: near-field beamfocusing, NOMA, hardware impairments in reconfigurable surfaces, and intelligent communication systems.

IRS-Assisted Near-Field NOMA with Imperfect Phase Compensation Ongoing

M.Tech (Research) + Ph.D. work, IIT Mandi

Large IRS panels at 30 GHz place nearby users in the radiative near field, where the panel can focus energy on a point instead of only steering it in a direction. This work asks when two users at the same angle but different distances can be served together with NOMA rather than OMA, while accounting for the phase error of low-cost phase shifters.
